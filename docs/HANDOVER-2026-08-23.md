### Local toolchain facts that cost hours to find

- **Use JDK 17, not Android Studio's bundled JDK.** Android Studio ships JBR 25;
  AGP supports 17 and 21. On 25 the CMake configure step fails with
  `[CXX1428] exception while building Json`, because a JEP 472 native-access
  warning is printed into output AGP parses as JSON. Temurin 17 is already at
  `~/.gradle/jdks/eclipse_adoptium-17-amd64-windows.2`.
- `ANDROID_HOME` must be set; `expo prebuild --clean` deletes `android/` and
  takes `local.properties` with it.
- `pnpm-workspace.yaml` sets `nodeLinker: hoisted`. React Native's native build
  resolves to real paths, and pnpm's default `.pnpm/<name>@<hash>/` nesting adds
  ~80 characters that a symlink hides from `ls` but not from CMake. This setting
  must live in `pnpm-workspace.yaml`; pnpm 11 silently ignores it in `.npmrc`.
- After any change to the package layout, delete
  `android/build/generated/autolinking` and `android/app/build/generated/autolinking`
  or Gradle resolves packages to paths that no longer exist.

## 2. The product pivot — read this before writing code

**`CLAUDE.md` has been rewritten and is the source of truth.** The previous
thesis — "Deepika provides the intelligence, the software provides memory and
structure" — is no longer the product. Do not restore it.

The app now **builds and adapts the plan itself**. A member signs up, answers a
short set of questions and a readiness screen, and receives a plan sized to her
time, ordered by her goals, and built around whatever she said hurts. It changes
from what she reports back.

**A coach is now an optional subscription** and outranks the software
completely. `doc.coaching.mode === "coached"` means the domains she owns, plus
anything she has published today, are left untouched and generation fills only
the rest. Absent means un-coached, which is the default.

The rule that governs all new work here: **rules decide, models phrase.**
Selection, progression, dose and every safety gate are deterministic and tested.
A model writes the one sentence explaining today, and runs the conversational
sign-up. Do not move a numeric decision into a prompt. If `OPENAI_API_KEY` is
absent the plan is byte-for-byte identical and the explanation falls back to a
written sentence.

### The one that would have bitten you

`dayOffset` is relative and **is re-based on read** by `lib/day-offset.ts`,
called from `lib/db.ts`. Nothing used to move those numbers, so a pulse recorded
on Monday still claimed to be today's on Tuesday. Anything new that stores a day
must either join that module's collection list or carry its own calendar date.
Prefer the calendar date — everything added since does.

### Server-derived state a client may never write

`app/api/state/route.ts` deliberately ignores these from the incoming document
and keeps the stored value:

- `doseSteps`, `pausedExerciseIds` — or an app could award itself a heavier dose
  and un-pause a movement that hurt
- `coaching` — a billing fact, not something a member's app asserts
- `readiness.outcome` — recomputed from the raw answers on every save, or a
  client posting `outcome: "clear"` unlocks movement for someone held back

Keep that property if you touch the merge.

## 5. The iOS work — where to pick it up

Your `d68ce8e` is intact: the Apple HealthKit read path in `mobile/src/health.ts`
(+330 lines), the platform-aware HRV handling that keeps Apple SDNN and Health
Connect RMSSD separate, the `ios-simulator` and `testflight` EAS profiles, and
the dev-build variant in `app.config.js`.

**What has changed underneath it since:**

1. `mobile/src/types.ts` gained `readiness`, `hydrationLogs`, `habits`,
   `habitLogs`, `doseSteps`, `pausedExerciseIds`, `planGeneratedOn`, and the
   circle types. `MemberDoc` is wider than you left it.
2. Three native modules were added — `expo-notifications`, `async-storage`,
   `netinfo` — plus `expo-location`. **Both async-storage and netinfo `throw` at
   module evaluation** when their native side is missing, so they are required
   lazily behind guards in `src/storage.ts` and `src/net.ts`. Do the same for
   anything new, or a stale build red-screens on launch instead of degrading.
3. `app.json` gained `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` and `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`, and
   **blocks `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`**. That block is deliberate — the app
   coarsens position to a ~3km cell on the device and never sends anything more
   precise, so requesting fine location would be asking for accuracy we destroy.
   Do not remove it when adding iOS location handling; the iOS equivalent is
   `NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription` with the same honest wording.
4. `mobile/src/exerciseMedia.ts` returns `null` for movements without
   photography rather than defaulting to the chair squat. 27 of the 35 movements
   are awaiting images (`docs/EXERCISE-MEDIA-BRIEF.md`); the app shows written
   frame labels meanwhile.

**Untouched and still yours:** everything iOS-specific. No iOS native directory
exists (`ios/` is gitignored and generated on demand).

**Practical note:** iOS cannot be built locally on this Windows machine. It has
to go through EAS — `eas build --profile ios-simulator` or `--profile testflight`.

## 7. If you change one thing, do not change these

- The exercise library's safety tags. They were reviewed by a qualified
  exercise professional and cleared. Loosening one is a real-world decision.
- The circle projection in `lib/circle.ts`. It builds what one member sees of
  another field by field and never spreads a member document, so a field added
  to `MemberDoc` later cannot silently become visible to someone else. Eight
  tests exist only to hold that line.
- The coarse-location design. Position is destroyed on the device, buckets are
  reported instead of distances, and fine location is blocked.
- The tone rules. No streaks, no "missed", no failure states, no red. A test
  asserts no generated daily action contains "coach", "streak", "missed",
  "failed" or "behind".
