### Original scope

**47 movements needed photography**, of 55 in the library. Eight had it when
this was written.

## What each movement needs

**Five frames**, matching the `frames` labels listed under each movement below.
Those labels are already written and appear beneath the images in the app, so
the photograph has to show the thing the label names — frame 3 of a squat says
"Touch chair", so frame 3 shows contact with the chair, not a mid-descent shot
that happens to look better.

**Same model, same position, same crop across all five.** These read as a
sequence; a change of camera angle between frames makes them look like five
different exercises. Landscape, 1200px on the long edge, WebP at quality 82 —
the current set runs about 40KB per frame and that is the budget.

## Who is in them

Members are mostly women over forty, many of them beginners. The people in
these photographs should look like that — not models, not athletes, and not
noticeably younger or fitter than the person looking at them. The gym
movements are the temptation here: a sled push shot with a competitive athlete
teaches a beginner that the movement is not for her.

Ordinary clothes. A real room or a real gym floor. Nothing that reads as a
fitness advertisement.

## Priority

Start with **Group A**, which is every movement a member can do at home with
nothing or a chair. That is what almost everyone will be offered, and it is
the majority of the list. The gym and event equipment groups are needed only
by members training for an event, and none of it is reachable before week
seven of a programme.

## Delivery

One folder per movement, named by its `id`:

```
ex-sled-push/
  1.webp  2.webp  3.webp  4.webp  5.webp
```

They are wired up by adding a `media` key to the movement in
`lib/exercise-library.ts` and an entry in `mobile/src/exerciseMedia.ts`. A
movement with no media shows its written frame labels instead, which is why a
partial delivery is safe to ship.

## Before any of Group B is used

The event movements have not been through the professional exercise review
that the original library passed. Photographs do not change that — a sled push
should not reach a member until someone qualified has checked the movement and
its contraindication tags.
